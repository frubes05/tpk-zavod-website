What sits behind the numbersŠto stoji iza brojki

Three service lines, each with its own long-running register.Tri usluge, svaka sa svojim dugogodišnjim registrom.

MONITORING

Condition assessment and monitoring of equipment in service — including expertise on damage cause, repair-procedure definition and reconstruction management — behind more than 3,500 issued reports.Procjena stanja i praćenje opreme u eksploataciji — uključujući ekspertize uzroka oštećenja, definiranje postupaka sanacije i vođenje rekonstrukcija — iza čega stoji više od 3.500 izdanih elaborata.

NDT

Non-destructive testing (RT, UT, PA, MT, PT, VT, vacuum) in the lab or on site, carried out by inspectors certified to HRN EN ISO 9712 and SNT-TC-1A/CP-189, behind roughly 120,000 test reports.Ispitivanje bez razaranja (RT, UT, PA, MT, PT, VT, vakuumsko ispitivanje) u laboratoriju ili na terenu, koje provode ispitivači certificirani prema HRN EN ISO 9712 i SNT-TC-1A/CP-189 — iza čega stoji oko 120.000 izvještaja o ispitivanju.

CERTIFICATION

Certification of welders, welding procedures, and pressure equipment — 3,161 welder certificates and 757 welding-procedure certificates since 2008, plus 322 pressure-equipment certificates since 2010.Certificiranje zavarivača, postupaka zavarivanja i tlačne opreme — 3.161 certifikat zavarivača i 757 certifikata postupaka zavarivanja od 2008., uz 322 certifikata tlačne opreme od 2010.
