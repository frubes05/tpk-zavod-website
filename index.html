About TPKO nama

What began as the R&D department inside a steam boiler factory is today an independent institute — with the people, laboratories and mobile equipment to carry a piece of energy or process equipment from first drawing to certified, in-service asset.Ono što je počelo kao Odjel za razvoj unutar tvornice parnih kotlova, danas je samostalan zavod — s ljudima, laboratorijima i pokretnom opremom koji energetsku ili procesnu opremu prate od prve skice do certificiranog, pogonskog uređaja.
